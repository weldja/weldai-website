Free system check

Find out in a few minutes whether a PC you already own can run Weld AI.

Weld AI runs entirely on a PC in your office, AI model included. That is what keeps your documents private, and it is also why the PC matters. This page tells you what is needed and gives you a free script that checks it for you.

What the PC needs

MinimumRecommended
WindowsWindows 10 (21H2 or later)Windows 11
Memory (RAM)12 GB16 GB or more
Free disk space25 GB40 GB or more
Processor4 cores8 cores or more
Graphics cardNoneNVIDIA with 8 GB of memory or more
InternetNot needed to run. Used on install day only.

How fast will answers be?

The graphics card decides. These figures are for a typical question about real company documents.

Graphics cardWhat to expect
NVIDIA, 8 GB or moreRecommended. The target is first words within 10 seconds.
NVIDIA, 4 GBMeasured: first words after about 10 seconds, a full answer in about 14 seconds, a document summary in about 30 seconds.
No NVIDIA cardIt works, slowly. Answers take 20 to 60 seconds or more, and summaries take several minutes.

Answers appear word by word as they are written, so you start reading before the answer is finished. A question that has been asked before is answered at once.

Run the check

Run this on the PC where Weld AI would be installed. It takes under a minute.

Download weldai-diagnostics.ps1

  1. Save it to your Desktop. If your browser warns that the file could harm your device, choose Keep. Browsers show this warning for every PowerShell script.
  2. Right-click the file and choose "Run with PowerShell". If asked about execution policy, type R and press Enter. Administrator rights are not needed.
  3. Email the result. A file called weldai-diagnostics.zip appears on your Desktop. Send it to hello@weldai.uk with the subject System check, [your business name].
What the script does and does not do. It makes no changes to your PC and no internet connections. It does not open your documents or list your files. It records hardware, Windows and network settings only, such as memory, graphics card, free disk space and the names of any mapped network drives. The report is a plain text file inside the zip, so you can read every line before you send it.

Where are your documents?

Weld AI reads documents from up to three folders and keeps itself up to date as files are added, changed or removed. It never changes, moves or deletes your files.

Where the documents areWorks?
A folder on the PC (C:, D: and so on)Yes
A USB or external driveYes, if it stays connected
A OneDrive or SharePoint folder synced to the PCYes. Set the folder to "Always keep on this device". Weld AI reads the copy on your PC.
A network drive or file server (U:, \\server\share)Not directly. I set up an automatic nightly copy to a folder on the PC.

The first time Weld AI reads a large collection it can take hours, particularly on a PC without a graphics card. A 1,000-page manual is roughly 1,000 passages to read. You can ask questions while it works, and after that only new and changed files are read.

What to tell me

When you email the zip, a few lines on the following helps me prepare:

  • Roughly how many people will use it
  • Which folder or folders hold the documents, and roughly how many files
  • Whether different people should see different documents (for example, HR files for HR only)
  • Whether you have an IT person or IT company I should talk to
