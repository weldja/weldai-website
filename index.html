Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before booking a demo.

Does anything leave our office?

No. The AI model, the search index, your documents, your questions and the answers all stay on the PC in your office. Weld AI makes no internet connections and sends no usage data to anyone, including me. There is no cloud AI provider involved at all.

Does it need an internet connection?

Not to run. Once installed, Weld AI works with the internet unplugged, and your IT person can block the PC from the internet at the firewall. An internet connection is used on install day only, for the screen-share call and to download Docker Desktop.

What computer does it need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC that stays switched on, with 16 GB of memory and 40 GB of free disk space. An NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB of memory or more is recommended for quick answers. The free system check tells you in a few minutes whether a PC you already own will do.

Run the free system check →   IT setup guide →

How fast are the answers?

It depends on the graphics card, because the AI runs on your PC rather than in a data centre. On a PC with a 4 GB NVIDIA card, measured on real company documents, the first words appear after about 10 seconds and a full answer takes about 14 seconds. A card with 8 GB or more is recommended. Without a graphics card it still works, but answers take 20 to 60 seconds or more.

Can I trust the answers?

Every answer names the document and page it came from, so staff can check it before relying on it. If your documents do not cover the question, Weld AI says so instead of guessing. Like any AI it can still make mistakes, so important answers should be checked against the source.

What files can it read?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, HTML and plain text, plus scanned documents and images (read with OCR on your PC) and Outlook emails with their attachments. It can watch up to three folders and pick up new, changed and deleted files automatically.

Can we control who sees which documents?

Yes. Each person signs in with their own account. Documents are shared with groups, such as Partners or HR, and someone outside a group cannot search or get answers from its documents. Staff can also sign in with their existing Windows (Active Directory) account.

Does this help with UK GDPR and client confidentiality?

It removes the hardest part. Because nothing is sent to a third party, there is no AI provider acting as a data processor, no transfer of data outside the UK, and no question about your files being used to train someone else's model. You are still responsible for who has access and how you use it, so check it against your own policies.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your setup, so I quote after a short call and the free system check. Because there is no cloud AI provider, there are no per-question or per-user AI fees to pay anyone.
