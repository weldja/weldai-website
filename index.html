Common questions

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask before booking a demo.

Do my documents leave my server?

No. Your documents, the search index and your chat history stay on your server. To write an answer, Weld AI sends Anthropic's Claude your question, the recent conversation, the names of the files it quotes, and up to eight short excerpts with identifiers such as NI numbers, UTRs, bank details, emails and phone numbers removed. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and deletes it within 30 days.

What does it run on?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC, a Windows Server, or a Linux server you already have, with at least 8GB of RAM. We check compatibility with you before installing.

IT team? Full requirements are in the IT Setup Guide →
Do we need IT staff?

No. We install remotely over a Teams or Zoom screen-share, usually in under an hour. Day-to-day use needs no technical skill: staff open a browser and type a question.

What files does it read, and how do I add new ones?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV and plain text. To add a document, copy it into your documents folder and click Refresh. Only new or changed files are re-read, so it takes seconds.

Can it read our file server, OneDrive or SharePoint?

Yes. Documents can be on a local drive, a synced OneDrive or SharePoint folder, or a file server. Where your files live decides which machine we install on, and we agree that with you before the install.

How much does it cost?

Installation is £350, done remotely and including 30 days of support. The only ongoing cost is AI usage, typically £2–5 per month for a small firm, paid directly to the AI provider. There is no monthly subscription to Weld AI.
