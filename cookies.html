Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Weld AI · York, England

Summary: This website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and it does not need cookies to work.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device. They are often used to remember you between visits or to track what you do.

Cookies on this website

This website does not use any of the following:

  • analytics or tracking cookies, such as Google Analytics
  • advertising or marketing cookies
  • social media tracking cookies

The site relies on two outside services to work: Vercel hosts the pages and Formspree delivers the enquiry form. Each one sees your IP address when your browser contacts it. The privacy policy has the details.

The Weld AI software (the installed product)

The Weld AI software installed on your own PC uses a single sign-in cookie so that you stay signed in while you use it. That cookie is set by your own PC and is only ever sent back to your own PC. It never reaches me or any third party.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser's settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the most recent update.
