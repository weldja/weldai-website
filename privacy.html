Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Weld AI · York, England

Plain English summary: The Weld AI software runs entirely on your own PC. Your documents, the search index, your questions and the answers never leave it. No cloud AI service is involved and the software sends nothing to me or to anyone else. Separately, when you contact me through this website I collect only the details needed to reply.

1. Who I am

Weld AI is the trading name of James Weld, a sole trader based in York, England. I supply and install private, on-premise AI document search software for small businesses. In this policy "I" and "me" mean James Weld trading as Weld AI.

Contact: hello@weldai.uk

2. How the Weld AI software handles your data

The Weld AI software is installed on, and runs entirely on, your own PC. This includes the AI model that writes the answers.

The software makes no internet connections when it runs. It works with the PC disconnected from the internet. I have no access to your installation, your documents, your questions or your accounts unless you choose to show them to me during a support session.

3. No third-party AI provider

Weld AI does not use a cloud AI service. The language model and the search model are open-source models that are installed on your PC with the software and run there. This means:

  • no AI provider acts as a data processor for your documents or questions
  • no data is transferred outside the UK by the software
  • your data cannot be used to train anyone's AI model, because it is never sent to anyone
You remain the data controller. Because the software runs on your PC, you decide which documents it indexes and who can sign in. You are responsible for the security of the PC, for backups, and for making sure your use of the software fits your own confidentiality and data protection obligations. Other software on the same PC, such as Windows, Docker Desktop, OneDrive or antivirus tools, is not part of Weld AI and has its own privacy terms.

4. Remote installation and support

Installation and support are normally carried out over a screen-share call that you start and can end at any time. During a session I can see whatever is on the shared screen. I do not copy, download or keep your documents. If I need a log file or a system check report to diagnose a problem, I will ask you to send it, and you can read it first.

5. Personal data I collect about you

When you use this website or contact me, I may collect:

  • your name, email address and business name, and anything you write in the enquiry form
  • emails you send me
  • a system check report, if you choose to email one. It contains hardware, Windows and network settings for one PC.

I do not take card payments through this website.

6. Services this website uses

  • Formspree delivers the enquiry form to my inbox. What you type into the form passes through Formspree's servers.
  • Vercel hosts this website and, like any web host, processes your IP address in order to serve the pages.

The fonts are served from this website itself, not from Google. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. See the cookie policy.

7. How I use your data

  • to reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for
  • to send messages about a service you have bought

I will never sell your personal data or use it for unsolicited marketing.

8. Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

  • Contract: processing needed to provide services you have requested
  • Legitimate interests: replying to enquiries and running the business
  • Consent: where you have explicitly opted in

9. Data retention

I keep enquiry details for up to 2 years. Emails relating to a contract are kept for up to 6 years for legal and accounting purposes.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to how it is used. To use any of these rights, email hello@weldai.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
